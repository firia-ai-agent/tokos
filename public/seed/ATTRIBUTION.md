# Seed provider headshots

The demo tenants need a real face on `/p/[slug]`. These are photographic headshots under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) — free to use commercially, no permission
needed, attribution not required but given here so the provenance of every binary in this
repo is checkable.

Both files were downloaded at 800×800 (`fit=crop`) and are served through the same upload
path a doula's own photo takes; see `seedProviderPhoto` in `scripts/seed.ts`.

Sam's photo was swapped in TOK-41. The previous one was a shirt-and-tie corporate headshot
under flat office light: accurate to a consultancy, wrong for the person who sits with you
through a long night. The brief for a replacement is warm natural light, an unforced smile,
and eye contact — a family should meet a person, not a stock photo.

The people pictured are not Maya Chen or Sam Ortega — those are fictional demo providers.
Do not reuse these images to imply a real endorsement.
